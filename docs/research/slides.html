Background: The Universal Challenge

Universities

Coordinating 100+ courses, 40 instructors, and capacity-constrained rooms with overlapping availability.

Tuition Centers

Managing 50-100 weekly classes with part-time staff and specific teacher/student preferences.

Corporate

Optimizing 20-50 cross-department meetings and specialized conference room resources.

Current Reality: Manual scheduling takes 5-30 hours per cycle and is highly error-prone with no optimization of workload balance or preferences.

02

Problem Statement

  • Time-Consuming: 5-30 hours per cycle in manual spreadsheet management.
  • High Error Rate: Frequent double-bookings and capacity violations.
  • Poor Change Control: Rescheduling causes cascading conflicts across the entire system.
  • No Optimization: Inability to balance workloads or honor soft constraints mathematically.

"How can we automate constraint-based scheduling across different domains to eliminate conflicts, save time, and optimize resource utilization?"

The Solution Gap

No existing tool provides a mathematical guarantee of conflict-free scheduling with domain flexibility.

03

Project Objectives

Development Goals

  • Design a universal automated system using Constraint Programming.
  • Leverage Google OR-Tools for optimal solution generation.
  • Create domain-specific templates (Edu, Corporate, Tuition).

Success Criteria

  • Zero scheduling conflicts (Mathematically guaranteed).
  • Generation time < 30s for 100+ items.
  • 90%+ time reduction compared to manual methods.
