BackgroundIn academic institutions, creating course timetables is a complex and time-consuming task that requires coordinating multiple constraints such as instructor availability, classroom capacity, course requirements, and institutional policies. Currently, many departments rely on manual scheduling or spreadsheet-based approaches, which are error-prone, difficult to maintain, and inefficient when changes occur.As the number of courses, instructors, and constraints increases, the timetabling problem becomes a combinatorial optimization challenge. Manual approaches struggle to guarantee conflict-free schedules and to satisfy preferences such as preferred teaching times or balanced workloads. Therefore, there is a strong need for an automated timetabling system that can systematically handle constraints, generate valid schedules, and optimize timetable quality.5. Objectives*  To design and develop an automated course timetabling system using constraint programming techniques.*  To apply Google OR-Tools to generate conflict-free and optimized timetables based on defined hard and soft constraints.*  To provide an administrative interface for managing courses, instructors, rooms, and scheduling rules.6. Scopes*  Develop a web-based application for academic timetabling management.*  Model hard constraints such as:* No instructor teaching overlapping classes* Room capacity and availability* No student group schedule conflicts*  Model soft constraints such as:* Instructor time preferences* Balanced distribution of classes*  Implement the scheduling engine using Google OR-Tools (Constraint Programming).*  Generate and visualize timetable results in a structured and user-friendly format.*  Focus only on timetable generation and management (no AI agents or NLP interfaces in this phase).7. Expected Benefits*  Reduced time and effort required to create academic timetables.*  Elimination of scheduling conflicts through systematic constraint validation.*  Improved timetable quality by optimizing preferences and workload balance.*  Increased flexibility in handling timetable changes compared to manual methods.*  Practical experience in applying optimization and constraint programming to real-world problems.8.
